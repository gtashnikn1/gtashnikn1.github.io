About

whoami / engineering philosophy

I am a DevOps and cloud engineer focused on researching and applying tools that make infrastructure simpler, safer, and easier to operate. My work sits at the intersection of Linux systems, cloud workflows, automation, containers, and security. I care about solving the bottleneck in front of the team, shipping useful systems early, and scaling only when real demand calls for it.
